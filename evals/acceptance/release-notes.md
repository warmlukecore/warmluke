# Release notes: Luke, 12–16 October (draft)

What changed for the owner in the first half of the sprint, in their terms. The scorecard is
`baseline.md` beside this page; the half-hour owner session is `owner-session.md`.

## Luke finishes what he starts

- Every task says where it stands: working, needs your answer, built, failed, stopped.
- A turn that did not finish offers **Try again**, which sends the same words, even after a reload.
- A design is built once: a second press or a retry never makes a second copy.
- When a design is refused, Luke may look the store up again before he fixes it, and never sends
  the same refused reply twice.
- A build is done when what you agreed actually comes true on your data, not when its JSON passes.

## Luke knows the business, and keeps it current

- What Luke knows has a kind, a topic and who said it: you, your data, a guess, or your website.
  A correction replaces the old line; a guess not confirmed in a month is let go.
- Who does what and how long a restock takes are asked once (onboarding and Settings) and used
  everywhere; teammates' chats read the operating facts, never the owner's private ones.
- Luke reads your website's returns, shipping, About and FAQ pages, as the site's own sentences.
  Where you told Luke something different, both are kept and Luke asks which holds.
- Luke says what of the store he has read and what is still coming in; a page not found is never
  taken for a policy you do not have.

## Investigations over every row

- RTO is compared like with like: the last N days of shipments beside the N before, each rate over
  the shipments that settled, broken down by courier, payment, state or NDR reason.
- How long stock lasts, variant by variant, with the day to reorder by against your restock time.
- Ads beside the stock they sell: a campaign's spend next to what its own orders bought, and
  whether that is running out. Budgets stay yours.
- Any of the store's lists can be counted exactly, never from a page of it.

## While you are away

- The Overview opens on today: what was built, what Luke is watching, what needs you.
- Watches for RTO rising, ads spending without sales, and ads on stock running out, beside running
  low, late to send and returns. Each is off until you give it its numbers; each alert says the next
  step and whose job it is.
- Asked the same thing on different days, Luke answers and offers once to keep it as a view.

## The first conversation

- The chat is usable at once (about 1–1.7 seconds measured on the dev server) while the store,
  the website and Luke's hello are still coming; you can type before he speaks.
- The first meeting asks how your days go, one question at a time, and **Go to store** works from
  the first screen, in any words.

## Open

- **Paid acceptance runs** (25 cases × 3): wait for a budget. Every check they stand on passes.
- **Owner session** (`owner-session.md`): yours to run, 30 minutes, no developer.
- **Decisions:** stock auto-send (the sprint says stock is never changed on Luke's say; 0195 lets
  an owner turn it on), the live ad account token, stock by SKU questions 3 and 5.
- **Not built:** a model looking into a new alert on its own (it costs on production), Shopify's
  own policy fields (needs a new permission and every store to reconnect; the public policy pages
  are read instead).
