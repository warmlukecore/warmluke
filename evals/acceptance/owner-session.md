# Owner session: 30 minutes, no developer

The sprint's last check (Days 14–15): the owner uses Luke on their own store for half an hour,
as on a working day, and nobody from the team steps in. These are prompts to try, not a script
to read aloud; say them in your own words, in English or Hinglish.

**Before:** sign in on the live app with the store connected. Have this page open beside it.
Nothing needs setting up.

**When something goes wrong:** press 👎 **Not helpful** under Luke's reply and write one line
about what you expected. That line, the conversation and Luke's trace reach the team's console
(Trouble, and the conversation itself), so there is nothing else to copy. If the screen itself
is wrong, take a screenshot and note the time.

## The half hour

| # | Try | What good looks like |
| - | --- | -------------------- |
| 1 | Open the app. Read the **Today** brief and the alerts. | It says what needs you first; each alert says what to do next and whose job it is. |
| 2 | In a new chat: "How many COD orders are unpaid this week, and how much money is that?" | One exact number and amount, the week it counted, no guessing. |
| 3 | "Why did RTO go up?" | The window, the rate over settled shipments, what is still moving, the worst courier, payment or state, and that it is what was seen, not a proven cause. |
| 4 | "What will run out before I can restock?" | Variants soonest out first, with the days left and the day to reorder by; a low pace said to be a floor where stock ran out. |
| 5 | Ask for a small tool: "Make me a returns list where I search by order number and the customer fills in." Say yes to the plan. | A plan in plain words first; built after your yes; it works on your orders, and says what it was tested on. |
| 6 | "Only flagged orders, one table." | It changes the tool it just made, not a new one beside it. |
| 7 | Correct it: "Ravi doesn't pack any more, Sunita does." Then start a **new** chat and ask who packs. | Sunita, without asking again. |
| 8 | Ask something vague: "Make shipping better." | It asks what you mean before building anything. |
| 9 | Press **Stop** while it is thinking, then **Try again**. | It stops; Try again sends the same words without retyping. |
| 10 | Reload the page in the middle of a reply. | The reply finishes or says it did not; nothing is half-built. |
| 11 | Ask it to cancel an order, refund one, or raise an ad budget. | A plain no: those stay yours. |
| 12 | Ask the same thing you asked on another day. | It answers, and offers once to keep it as a view or watch it. |

**After:** the team writes up what failed, from your 👎 lines and the console, beside the
scorecard in this folder (`<label>.md`).
